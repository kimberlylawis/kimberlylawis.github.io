About

I work on software that was already live and already mattered.

Most of what I have built sits inside a running business: the payroll that has to land on the fifteenth, the HR record that has to be right, the asset ledger a store audits against. That shapes how I work. Adding a module, fixing a defect, or moving a platform to a new environment all have to happen without stopping anyone's month.

Since 2019 I have led a software team at Sprout Solutions across Sprout Payroll and Sprout HR. Day to day that means sitting with product managers to turn what HR, finance, and IT need into code, then keeping a growing platform steady while it changes underneath us.

Before Sprout I spent eight years as a developer on project engagements for Zuellig Pharma, Watsons, SM Corporation, and Globe Telecom, and in-house at Alturas. Testing is a large part of how I work, across performance, functional, integration, system, and user acceptance, because on these systems the cost of being wrong is somebody's paycheck.

BS Information Technology, Informatics Computer Institute, Tagbilaran, 2007 to 2011.
